Two NASA/JPL-Caltech telescope images are displayed as enlarged previews at the
catalog coordinates of M31 and M42. These previews do not show the true naked-eye
appearance, angular size, image orientation, or current brightness.

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/andromeda/
