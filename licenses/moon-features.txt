Lunar landmark selection: Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature, maintained by
the USGS Astrogeology Science Center on behalf of IAU WGPSN with NASA support.
Official source page: https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/Page/MOON/target
Data source: https://asc-planetarynames-data.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/MOON_nomenclature_center_pts.kmz
Snapshot and original KMZ hash: moon-features-source.json.

Forty adopted names, reference center coordinates and directory diameters are
selected from the official lunar KMZ. Only numerical facts and feature names
are bundled. Coordinates and diameters are rounded to two decimals; longitude
is east-positive and normalized to -180..180. The original data is in the
LOLA 2011 control network. Marker dots denote directory reference centers,
not terrain boundaries, landing positions or pixel-exact scientific alignment.
English names are the directory names. Chinese display translations are by
Starlight and are not claimed as official IAU translations.
No USGS, IAU or NASA affiliation or endorsement.

Each source record is available at https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/Feature/ID
using the numeric ID stored in moon-features-data.js.
The regeneration helper tools/prepare_moon_features.py accepts the downloaded
official KMZ via --input or retrieves the current official snapshot. Regeneration
may change coordinates and requires review; it is not run automatically in CI.
