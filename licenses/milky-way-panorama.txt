Runtime changes: reprojected onto the chart using galactic coordinates;
brightness and latitude/horizon fading applied by a GPU shader. A smaller
texture is used only when the device cannot upload the original size.
This is a historical long-exposure photographic backdrop, not a live sky,
a visibility forecast, or a replacement for the catalog star positions.
The source mosaic contains stars and Solar System objects from its capture.
